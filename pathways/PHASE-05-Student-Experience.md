# PHASE 05 — Student Experience

## Objective
Design the learner journey from assessment to an informed senior school decision.

## Entry Point
Navigation:
My Future → Pathway Decision Centre

## Journey
1. Welcome
2. View current pathway recommendation
3. Understand why it was recommended
4. Explore alternative pathways
5. Explore careers
6. Compare subject combinations
7. Discover matching senior schools
8. Save favourites
9. Discuss with parent
10. Receive counsellor feedback
11. Finalize personal decision plan

## Core Screens
- Dashboard
- My Pathway
- Career Explorer
- Subject Combinations
- School Matches
- Saved Schools
- Action Plan
- Timeline

## Notifications
- New recommendation
- Parent feedback received
- Counsellor comments
- Approval status updates

## UX Principles
- Explain every score.
- Show confidence, not hidden formulas.
- Encourage exploration before commitment.
- Preserve history of recommendations.
