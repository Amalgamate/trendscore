# PHASE 00 — Vision

## Mission

Empower every Kenyan learner to make confident, evidence-based pathway and senior school decisions through a collaborative platform connecting learners, parents, counsellors and schools.

## Product Vision

TrendScore will become the trusted decision-support platform that guides learners from assessment to pathway, subject combination, career exploration and senior school selection.

## Success Principles

- Explainable recommendations
- Parent and learner collaboration
- Professional counsellor oversight
- Ministry-aligned pathway guidance
- Data-driven continuous improvement

## Scope

This module covers:

- Pathway recommendations
- Subject combinations
- Career exploration
- Senior school matching
- Counsellor workflows
- Parent participation
- Recommendation reports

## Out of Scope

- Admissions management
- Student placement by government
- Learning Management functionality
- School ERP operations

## Deliverables

The remaining phases define architecture, data models, user journeys, implementation and rollout.
