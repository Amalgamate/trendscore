# PHASE 01 — Product Principles

## Purpose
Define the non-negotiable principles that guide every design and engineering decision.

## Core Principles
1. Explain every recommendation.
2. Keep the learner at the centre.
3. Parents collaborate, not override.
4. Counsellors provide professional oversight.
5. AI assists; humans decide.
6. Every recommendation is evidence-based.
7. Preserve learner choice until formal approval.
8. Design for Kenyan CBC first.
9. Reuse existing TrendScore capabilities before creating new ones.
10. Build for future expansion (universities, TVET, scholarships).

## Success Criteria
- Transparent recommendations
- High parent participation
- Consistent counselling workflows
- Configurable rules
- Modular architecture

## Acceptance Checklist
- Every feature maps back to at least one principle.
- No feature violates learner transparency.
- No hidden scoring or unexplained AI decisions.
