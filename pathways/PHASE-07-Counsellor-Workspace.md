# PHASE 07 — Counsellor Workspace

## Objective
Provide counsellors with a professional workspace to guide learners from recommendation to approved decision.

## Dashboard
- Students awaiting review
- Students requiring intervention
- Parent/student disagreements
- Pathway distribution
- Upcoming counselling sessions

## Learner Workspace
- Academic profile
- TrendScore recommendation
- Supporting evidence
- Parent preferences
- Career interests
- Recommended subject combinations
- Suggested senior schools
- Counsellor notes

## Actions
- Add observations
- Recommend changes
- Schedule counselling
- Approve decision plan
- Return for revision
- Lock completed plans

## Reports
- Class summaries
- School readiness
- Intervention history
- Approval progress

## Permissions
Counsellors cannot alter assessment scores but can approve, reject, annotate and guide pathway decisions.
