## Rules
- Keep the learner at the centre.
- Parents collaborate but do not silently override.
- Counsellors provide professional review.
- Explain every recommendation.
- Distinguish fit from admission probability.
- Distinguish official, verified and community-reported data.
- Do not expose raw scoring formulas by default.
- Do not invent architecture that conflicts with the repository.

## Workflow
1. Review the active SPEC.
2. Define every screen and state.
3. Define user actions and outcomes.
4. Define warnings, confirmations, errors and empty states.
5. Identify edge cases.
6. Produce acceptance criteria.
7. Review implemented UX.
